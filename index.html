      How Starkeys teaches
      • One 10-minute session a day: warm-up, new keys, a scored typing round, then a game. Short daily practice beats long weekly sessions.
      • Keys arrive two at a time, starting on the home row (F and J bumps), with every finger colour-coded.
      • Accuracy first: a mission unlocks the next one at 90% accuracy. After all keys are learned, Speed Run missions require 15, 20, 25, 30, 35 and 40 WPM at 92% accuracy.
      • From mission 7 the on-screen keyboard fades, and only lights up if your child hesitates. This builds the habit of not looking down.
